      Frequently asked questions.

      What can I store?

      Furniture, boxes, appliances, bikes, archives, business stock. Perishable food, dangerous or flammable goods and animals are not allowed.

      Do I need insurance?

      Your belongings must be insured. Choose one of our plans when you book, or keep your home insurance if it covers storage.

      When can I move in?

      On the date you choose, from the day after booking. Your access code arrives by email the day before.

      How do I cancel?

      From your account or at reception, giving 15 days' notice before the end of the month.
